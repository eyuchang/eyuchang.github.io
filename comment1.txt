  Because of the large number and volumneous nature of multimedia data,
  a multimedia system must use magnetic disks for economical storage.  

o Why the problem is important and urgent?

  Unfortunately, the disk latency continues to be the dominant 
  bottleneck of any system using disks, and the performance gap 
  between IO and CPU continues to be widened.
