Hi Ken,

While reading the IGERT proposal, I am encouraged that
your group is also actively working on the clustering/indexing
problem.  I believe your group will do a superb job.

Your reference on [Cheng, Ferhatosmanoglu]'s work, however,
may not be the earlies work on clustering/indexing.  The earliest
works in the DB community are:

Clindex, C. Li, E. Chang. H. Garcia-Molina, etc., Stanford Technical
Report, 1998.

Scaling EM (Expectation-Maximization) Clustering to Large Databases,
Paul S. Bradley and Usama M. Fayyad and Cory A. Reina, Microsoft
Technical Report, 1998.

Also, since you are on Kingshy's PhD committee, and she has been
working on this problem for a year with a couple of paper written,
do you think there could a possibility for collaboration in
solving this challenging problem?

Thank you for your kind advice.
Ed
