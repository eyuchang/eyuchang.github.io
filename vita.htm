Research Experience

  • Research Assistant, Computer Science Department, Stanford University, 1992-present
    Advisor: Professor Hector Garcia-Molina
  • Developed algorithms to consistently integrate data and incrementally incorporate changes to a data warehouse. The family of algorithms designed covers a wide variety of update and transaction scenarios at both the sources and the warehouse.
  • Implemented (together with other students) a data warehouse prototype WHIPS using CORBA (multiple-language object interfaces). WHIPS maintains multiple materialized views consistently and concurrently, with heterogeneous data sources such as commercial database systems, flat files, legacy systems and real-time data streams.
  • Studied the performance of the WHIPS system, including the performance of view maintenance algorithms under various update scenarios and system load.
  • Designed incremental view maintenance algorithms to handle graph structured data.
  • Studied intelligent, example-based text classification techniques.
  • Developed, implemented and tested parallel sorting algorithms on Stanford shared memory DASH parallel machine and Intel iPSC/860.
  • Summer Intern, Intel Corporation, Santa Clara, California,1993
    Mentor: Dr. Dan Seligson.
  • Worked on motion estimation of movies and parallel MPEG programs. Implemented an algorithm as part of a research prototype to estimate motion of MPEG compressed movie frames, then parallelized the algorithm. Evaluated different hardware approaches to solve the same problem.
  • Research Assistant, SUNY at Stony Brook, New York, 1990-1991
    Advisor: Professor Yuefan Deng
  • Conducted parallel programming, debugging and performance analysis of FronTier, a fluid front tracking system, using Intel iPSC/860. Designed an innovative algorithm to do dynamic domain decomposition and successfully applied it to the 3D fluid interface problem. Part of the research results are presented in the Parallel Circus meeting at Oak Ridge, 1991.
  • Research Assistant, Lab of Artificial Intelligence, Tsinghua University, Beijing, P.R.China 1988-1990
    Advisor: Professor Bo Zhang
  • Developed software for robot motion planning.
  • Designed and implemented algorithms for office automation. The algorithms efficiently managed daily documents passed among offices in the computer science department, using basic workflow management techniques.
