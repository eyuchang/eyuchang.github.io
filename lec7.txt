o Perspective

  - An image is a 2D canvas that contains 3D information

    the sizes, shapes and distance of depicted objects.
  - How the real world differs from the depicted world?

  - the ubiquitous and compelling analogy between the eye and
    the camera has ensured a central role for the retinal image
  - the retinal images formed in our eyes: what we see is
    not the world but its images!!!

  - constructivist theory
    * poor/ambiguous images provide cues or clues to the 
      layout of the scene depicted
  - ecological approach
    * perception depends on  the richly informative spatiotemporal
      optical structures in the transforming optical array of an 
      active, exploring perceiver.

o Theoretical approach
  - Alberti:  
    * outline a rectangle as an open window through which the
      objects to be painted are seen, plane P
    * determine the height of a man, H (horizon)
    * divide the base into scales (braccia, one braccio = 1/3 man height)
    * decide the point, V, at which the centric ray strikes the picture
    * join the braccio divisions to the perspective focus, V, to give
      the orthogonals
    ** positions of depth 
    * in side elevation, lines are drawn from braccio divisions behind
      P ot the eye at E.  the points of intersection on P are noted
    * diagonals drawn through the projected square meets precisely
      at two lateral points, Z1 and Z2.
    * ZV = EV
