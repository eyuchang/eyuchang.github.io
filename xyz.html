Overview

In the 2023 edition of this course, we plan to use ChatGPT and GPT APIs to design and implement a care-giving agent to provide health care. The students will learn the latest applied AI research in disease diagnosis and treatment recommendations, and use large pre-trained language models such as ChatGPT to implement a healthcare virtual agent.
