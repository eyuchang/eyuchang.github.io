What is color?

o Our eyes detect only a very narrow band of electromagnetic 
  spectrum. (Electromagnetic spectrum is a range of energy
  of varying wavelengths)
  Human eyes are receptive only to narrow wavelengths range 
  from 350 nm to 750 nm. 

  red orange yellow green blue indigo violet

o How do we perceive color?

  5 major components in our eyes

  - Light beams enter the eye via cornea, which serves as 
    a curved exterior lens that gathers and focuses the
    entering light. 
  - Light passes through pupil and reaches interior lens.
  - The iris adjusts to allow proper amount of light 
    to reach the interior lens.
  - The lens adjusts the amount of curvature to focus on 
    objects at different distances...
    (resolution...)

  - The light finally reaches retina
  - the electromagnetic light energy transduced into
    neural electrochemical impulses.
  - Two kinds of receptive cells
    * 6 - 8M cones
    * 120M rods
  - finally ganglion cells (10 M)

o Color is an entirely subjective experience

  - The colors of an object decided by the light reflect from the
    objects that we are looking at.
  - Colors are a reaction of our nervous systems to particular
    wavelengths of the visible spectrum.
  - The sensation of color lies in the interaction between
    light and the nervous system...

Color History:

o From the later stage of Aristo'telian tradition to the
  time of Newton, it was believed that the colors were 
  quantifiable and controllable.

o There is a progressive clarification of what we may call the
  three dimensions of color: Hue, saturation and tone...

o Aristotle:
  7 colors: black, red, yellow and green
          + lime white, blue and kind of yellow...

o Renaissance

  - Cennini
    * pick Aristotelian colors
    * mix colors
    * toning the colors with Cangianti/Changeant colors
    * Cangianti: brighter colors such as while and yellow -> grace
    * Changeant: darker colors in the shadows

    ** Colors are exploit for aes'thetical expression independent
       of natura'listic consideration

  - Alberti
    * Searching for a rational base for achieving artistic effects. 
    * How color is made from a mixture of the quality of the object
      rare/dense, hot/dry, cold/wet?
    * fire -> red...  ice -> white/blue air -> blue/grey
