Ed:

Attached is a set of claims that have been amended pursuant to our
conversation on Friday, October 12th.  Please review the claims, and let me
know what you think.

Once the claims are finalized (and if you agree that the attached claims are
acceptable, then they will constitute the finalized claims.), they will
serve as a template for modifications to the MEGA paper to transform it into
a utility patent application.  As you know, that paper already has been
filed as part of a provisional patent application.

FIRST REQUEST: Also, I look forward to receiving the additional descriptive
information that we discussed.  For instance, it would be useful to have
examples of at least a simple k-CNF expression and a simple k-DNF expression
and several sample images and corresponding sample expressions.  In that
example, there should be some correlation of terms in the k-CNF expression
to image features and of terms in the k-DNF expression to image features,
and of terms in the sample expressions to image features.  I know that such
an example can easily become unwieldy.  So, a simple example is acceptable.

SECOND REQUEST: It would be a good idea to illustrate the identification of
contradictions between sample expressions and the k-CNF (and the absence of
contradictions between the sample expressions and the k-DNF?) through an
example in which some terms of the k-CNF are contradicted by at least the
prescribed number of sample expressions and other terms of the k-CNF are
contradicted by fewer than the prescribed number of sample expressions.  An
example providing an analogous example for the k-DNF also would be a good
idea.  See claims 10-11. 

THIRD REQUEST: It would be a good idea to illustrate the sample selection
process through an example in which at least one sample expression is
selected because it has exactly the prescribed number of terms that
contradict the k-CNF (and that do not contradict the k-DNF????), and at
least one sample expression is rejected because it has fewer than the
prescribed number of terms that contradict the k-CNF (and and that do not
contradict the  k-DNF?),  and at least one sample expression is rejected
because it has more than the prescribed number of terms contradict the k-CNF
(and and that do not contradict the k-DNF?).  See claims 12-15. 

FOURTH REQUEST: Also, it would be helpful to have an example of the division
of the k-CNF (and k-DNF?) into sub-group expressions without loss of
"feature information".  See claims 6 and 7.

FIFTH REQUEST: Also, it would be useful to have an example of
multi-resolution processing.  See claims 9 and 10.

SIXTH REQUEST: What are, "multiple pre-clustered sets of sample images"?
See claim 16.

Please do not hesitate to contact me if you would like to discuss any of
these issues.  I look forward to speaking with you soon.

Best Regards,

Steve
