The first version I sent out last night was supposed to be a somewhat
different motivation for the pre-proposal. This is an alternate version
i wrote up a couple of days ago but was not happy with. What would be
the best approach for introducing the challenges and making the
reviewers want to read on and ask us to submit a pre-proposal? The

Another approach to take would be to link this to BW requirements for
social needs that don't change with the economic tides (Medical, etc).

1.1 Motivation and Challenges (earlier version)
As the capability to generate and house massive amounts of data
globally continues to increase at unprecedented rates, the capability
for end users to access and interact with this data on a pervasive
scale remains elusive. From scientists to elementary school children,
there is a need to interact with each other and with these vast
storehouses of data, and the failure to provide true high bandwidth
information access has become the single most critical bottleneck to
pervasive deployment of broadband networks and generation of new
applications today. The impact of removing this bottleneck will be to
change the way we educate and learn, conduct collaborative science and
engineering, and grow new economically stimulating applications and
businesses that can reach all segments of society.
The proposed Project is our vision of how to expand the frontiers of IT
and its applications by enabling pervasive interaction with
Gigabit-per-Second optical information. We see the future as not just
about accessing high bandwidth data but being interactive with the data
where large amounts of information will be sent out as well as
received, in contrast to many of today’s asymmetric broadband
solutions. We realize that at the highly pervasive scale (i.e. to every
home user), this technology may initially lead to symmetrical 100 Mp/s
with a roadmap to Gb/s.The symmetrical flow of Gigabit-per-Second data
will require the bandwidth supplied by optical fibers and the fiber
wiring itself to be pervasive.  The question whether high bandwidth
will be needed on a pervasive basis and on a symmetrical basis is an
open issue. There are indicators for scientific applications like iGrid
2002 (we could put some quotes in here for BW needs from the actual NSF
studies).
Delivery of this technology on a mass scale will not be possible
without significant advances in photonic integration akin to the
revolution in electronic integration in the 1960s. However, the
development of integrated photonic technologies cannot be done in a
vacuum but must be done in concert with other circuit technologies,
with knowledge of how this technology will interplay with the network
architecture and it’s functions, the computing architecture and the end
user applications. It is equally important that the impact of pervasive
gigabit photons on the applications themselves and the way scientists,
engineering, students and the general public will do their work and
live their lives in the future.
