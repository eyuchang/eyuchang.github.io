6:00 PM

🚂 Arrival & Check-in

Arrive at Cologne Central Station (Hauptbahnhof). The cathedral is literally 2 minutes away! Check into your hotel or drop bags, then head straight to the Dom for your first impressive view.
💡 Tip: Cologne Central Station has lockers if you need to store luggage temporarily.
6:15 PM

⛪ First Cathedral View

Get your first look at the magnificent Gothic cathedral. Walk around the exterior, take photos, and soak in the impressive architecture. The interior stays open until 7:30 PM if you want a quick peek inside.
📸 Photo tip: Best exterior shots from the cathedral steps and nearby Roncalliplatz.
7:00 PM

🏛️ Old Town Evening Stroll

Wander through the charming Altstadt with its colorful medieval buildings, narrow cobblestone streets, and traditional shops. Perfect golden hour lighting for photos and getting oriented with the city layout.
🚶‍♂️ Route: Walk from Dom via Unter Goldschmied to Heumarkt, then circle back through side streets.
8:00 PM

🍺 Traditional Kölsch Experience

Dinner at a traditional brewhouse! Try authentic Kölsch beer (served in small 0.2L glasses) with local specialties like Himmel un Ääd (heaven and earth - mashed potatoes with apples and black pudding) or Halver Hahn (rye bread with cheese).
🍻 Recommendations: Früh am Dom, Gaffel Haus, or Peters Brauhaus for authentic atmosphere.
9:30 PM

🌉 Rhine River Evening Walk

End your evening with a peaceful stroll along the Rhine promenade. The cathedral looks stunning when illuminated, and you'll get beautiful views from the river embankment. Perfect way to wind down and plan tomorrow.
🌅 Best time: Stay until around 10 PM to see the cathedral's evening illumination.
9:00 AM

⛪ Cathedral Tower Climb

Start early with the 533-step climb up the south tower of Cologne Cathedral. The panoramic views over the city and Rhine are incredible, and you'll avoid the crowds by going first thing in the morning.
💪 Fitness tip: It's quite a workout! €6 entrance. Open from 9 AM.
10:30 AM

🎨 Museum Ludwig

Explore one of Europe's finest modern art collections. See works by Picasso, Warhol, Lichtenstein, and other masters. The museum is right next to the cathedral, making it perfectly convenient.
🎫 Cost: €13 adult ticket. Audio guide recommended for €4 extra.
12:30 PM

⛵ Rhine River Cruise

Take a relaxing 1-hour Rhine cruise to see Cologne from the water. Great views of the cathedral, bridges, and riverfront. Perfect lunch break with onboard café service available.
🚢 Departure: Boats leave hourly from near Hohenzollern Bridge. €15-20 per person.
2:30 PM

🛍️ Belgian Quarter Exploration

Discover Cologne's trendiest neighborhood! Browse unique boutiques, independent galleries, vintage shops, and cozy cafes. The area has a distinctly different vibe from the historic center.
☕ Coffee break: Try Café Extrablatt or Van Dyck for excellent coffee and people-watching.
4:00 PM

⛪ Great St. Martin Church

Visit this impressive Romanesque church with its distinctive cloverleaf choir and tower. Offers a nice architectural contrast to the Gothic cathedral and beautiful interior frescoes.
🏛️ Architecture: One of twelve Romanesque churches in Cologne's old town.
5:00 PM

🍫 Chocolate Museum

End your cultural day with something sweet! Learn about 3,000 years of chocolate history, watch chocolate being made, and enjoy generous tastings. The chocolate fountain is a highlight!
🍬 Sweet deal: €15 entrance includes tastings. Great gift shop for souvenirs.
6:30 PM

💝 Hohenzollern Bridge

Walk across the famous railway bridge covered with thousands of love locks. This is THE spot for cathedral photos, especially during golden hour. Watch trains pass beneath your feet!
📸 Photo magic: Best cathedral shots are from the middle of the bridge around sunset.
7:30 PM

🍺 Traditional Brewhouse Tour

Cap off your day with a mini brewhouse crawl! Visit 2-3 traditional brewhouses to compare different Kölsch varieties and enjoy hearty Rhine cuisine. Each brewery has its own character and recipes.
🍻 Route: Start at Früh am Dom, then Gaffel Haus, end at Peters Brauhaus for dinner.

🎯 Essential Tips

  • KölnCard: €9/day for public transport + museum discounts
  • Cathedral entry: Free, but tower climb costs €6
  • Kölsch culture: Beer comes in small 0.2L glasses, waiters keep bringing them!
  • Cash vs Card: Most places accept cards, but carry some euros
  • Language: English widely spoken in tourist areas
  • Sunday note: Many shops closed, but attractions and restaurants open

🚊 Getting Around

  • Walking: Most attractions within 15-min walk of cathedral
  • U-Bahn/Trams: Excellent network, tickets from €3
  • Rhine cruise: €15-20, departures every hour
  • Taxi/Uber: Available but walking is usually faster in center
  • Bike rental: KVB-Rad stations throughout city
  • Airport transfer: S-Bahn S13 direct to city center
