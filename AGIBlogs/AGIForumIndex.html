Only Popcorn Is Left Behind

I stopped going to theaters, never subscribed to Netflix, and now watch roughly two short movies a day on my iPhone. What exactly is disappearing—and what is left behind?

Los Angeles is debating how to save Hollywood production. But mobile short drama and generative AI may be changing the product itself: shorter stories, synthetic actors and scenes, instant localization, and an evening once built around a restaurant and a movie becoming entertainment delivered directly to the couch. Perhaps only the popcorn survives unchanged.

Alignment Is Not Authorization

What happens when the same AI can propose an action, interpret the rules, judge its own compliance, and execute the result?

Recent agent incidents expose a problem deeper than alignment: concentrated authority. This essay argues for a checks-and-balances architecture—separate executive, legislative, and judicial AI roles, auditable interaction without interference, and an external commit gate that turns reasoning into action only when authorization is explicit.
