Invariant Feature Extraction and Biased Statistical Inference for Video Surveillance
Review #1:
Key Contribution: This paper described a motion trajectory representation claimed to be invariant to 3D pose and the speed of motion. It then proposed a modified SVM for biased learning with experiments on detecting fire and smoke alarms.
Comments to Author: This paper is a mishmash of all sorts of things with bits of it interesting, especially on biased learning using modified SVM. I cannot see any relevance between the stuff on invariant motion descriptor and the SVM biased learning & the experiments represented on fire and smoke event detection has nothing to do with motion trajectories. As it stands, it cannot be accepted for any publication since it's simply not a coherent paper. I would want to take out the part on invariant motion descriptor (no relevance to the experiment and the rest of the paper and only stuck in from some past work for no apparent reason here)and focus the paper on biased learning using modified SVM.
