Knowledge Tools for Expediting New Drug Discovery and Development

Summary:

This project proposes to develop a visualization tool that
can help 1) explore in multiresolution silico analysis, and
2) rank ordering of NCEs in terms of their relative risk
of being rejected as an approvable new drug. The developed
framework will serve as a digital bridge between computerized
drug discovery and testing in humans.
